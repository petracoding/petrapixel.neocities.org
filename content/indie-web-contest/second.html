Petra's Second Indie Web Contest!

My last indie web contest was a celebration for me reaching 300 followers on Neocities. As I now have more than 1300 2000 followers I think it's time for another one!

I see so many beautiful, interesting, unique, and helpful websites on the indie web, and the point of this contest to celebrate them!

This contest is over. Check out the winners below!
Go here for the current contest.

Winners!

Congratulations to all winners! Winners should have received an email (check junk) or guestbook entry from me - please comment below if you haven't!

There were 175 total participants (see list below). Site links in the voting form were in randomized order (and winners were calculated using average points instead of total points) to achieve fairness.

Most Beautiful

These sites have received the highest average points based on how beautiful they are.

Best Content
